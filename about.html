About us

A Sabah company building for Sabah

Bytes IT Solution was founded in Kota Kinabalu in 2017. Nine years on, we are a trusted technology partner to five state agencies and to private clients across the region.

Who we are

From one licence to 35 projects

We started in January 2017 with a single trading licence for information technology development. Our first project was a boundary stone management system for Jabatan Tanah dan Ukur Sabah, and we still maintain the survey systems we built for them in 2018.

Since then our experienced team has delivered work across the public and private sectors: custom management systems, geospatial platforms, network infrastructure, hardware supply, and newer AI and extended reality technologies.

We take pride in delivering high-quality work and in being a company clients can rely on year after year. Our aim is simple: to meet each client's real needs and give them the best service we can.

Our vision
To be Sabah's most trusted technology partner, delivering exceptional service and innovative solutions our clients can depend on, year after year.
